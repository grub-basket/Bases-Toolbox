## 0.1.65 — Filter the history by source

- **Filter the bulk file change history by source.** A dropdown on the history view narrows the log to a single kind of operation. Pick **Kanban order** (which groups the card-ordering and its accompanying sort change into one option) for a kanban-scoped view of exactly what changed, with one-click reverts — or narrow to find & replace, merges, CSV imports, view/column-manager edits, and so on. The summary shows "N of M operations (filtered)", and "All sources" restores the full list. The dropdown only appears once there's more than one kind of operation in the log.
